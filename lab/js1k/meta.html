Code Golfing in JS1K

Javascript

The goal in the JS1K Javascript competition was to make a demo using only 1023 bytes of Javascript code. As you can imagine, one has to be very creative both in the idea and the implementation.

Crazy multiplayer 2-sided pong

Demo | Submission info

It is a spinning, 4-sided pong for two players (mouse and a,z keys), featuring effects, ball collisions and scoreboard. Also, the speed of the ball increased during the round.

PAC-MAN

Demo | Submission info

I wanted to preserve original Pac-Man map layout and gameplay feeling, which took too much space and got short of bytes for implementing the "power pellets" to eat the ghosts.

Ghost AI is obviously simple, and basically non-existent: a "pseudorandom" turn is picked from previous turn plus a counter.

Fluid solver (1872 Bytes)

I tried to squeeze Jos Stam's "Real-Time Fluid Dynamics for Games" implementation for JS1K, but I didn't manage to shrink it to 1024 Bytes without destroying it too much. I had a lot of fun implementing the algorithm, but could not present it to the competition.
