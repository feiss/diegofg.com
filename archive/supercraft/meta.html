WebVR content creation tool that allows users to create objects and export them to A-Frame projects.

It was done by Kevin Ngo and me. I was mostly involved in the UI and UX, programming support and content.

We wanted to provide a very lightweight (less than 10MB), very performant (suitable for low end VR headsets), and very easy to use, to welcome and encourage all VR users to be creative and build their own worlds. Pro artists can draw and model, and use VR tools like Quill, Sculpt VR, or Gravity Sketch, but non artists will be overwelmed. For this reason, we decided to provide huge constraints, like no textures and no shape modelling, just kitbash basic shapes with the basic transforms and by direct manipulation.

  • Very lightweight, almost instant load in the browser and the headset
  • Very performant, suitable for low end VR headsets
