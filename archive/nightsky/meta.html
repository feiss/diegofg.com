WebVR app to discover the stars and constellations. Design, 3D content and programming. Optimized for Oculus Quest. Astronomy programming by Diego Marcos.

The UX was quite simple and intuitive. When pointing towards the floor, the right controller was a regular flashlight to orient yourself in the dark. When pointing towards the sky, it became a laser pointer that you could use to point to a star and activate the constellation it belongs to. Also, technical information was displayed in the left hand panel.
