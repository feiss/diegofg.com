This was my first deep dive into Gaussian Splats, working for World Labs. My task was to improve some of the existing examples of their popular Spark.js library and create new ones. The focus was not to make outstanding and complicated scenes, but very simple ones, with clear and easy to read code that people can learn from.

I used many tools available at the moment for creating and training splats, the best for me were Colmap and Jawset Postshot.

Specifically, I:

  • created gaussian splats both from different sources: real life photos and videos, synthetic objects made in Blender, and AI generated meshes
  • created scripts and a Blender Add-on for speeding up the pipeline
  • designed the Spark logo and its Viewer
