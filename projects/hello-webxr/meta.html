This demo was aimed to celebrate the release of the WebXR v1.0 API.

It is designed as a playground where you can try different experiences and interactions in VR, and introduce newcomers to the VR world and its special language in a smooth, easy and nice way.

This experience was kind of state of the art back then, and it was featured and used in many schools as a reference due to its easy to follow code structure and the diversity of techniques and use cases covered.

You can read a post mortem article with some design and technical details.
